# Fictional weekly review / 虚构周报

All numbers and situations below are invented for illustration. They are not the repository owner's data, clinical thresholds, or expected outcomes.

以下全部数字与情境均为虚构，不是仓库所有者的数据、临床阈值或效果承诺。

## English

**The main change was a longer weekend session, alongside shorter measured sleep.** Your training frequency stayed similar. That supports reviewing recovery before adding more training next week; it does not establish overtraining.

Period: 5–11 October 2026, Asia/Shanghai. Training records cover all seven days; sleep is available for six nights. The comparison period is 7 September–4 October. Today's partial data is excluded.

| Observation | Review week | Previous four weeks |
|---|---|---|
| Training duration | 240 min | 180 min/week on average |
| Sessions | 4 | 4/week on average |
| Longest session | 120 min hike | Median weekly longest session: 75 min |
| Median measured sleep | 6 h 40 min (6/7 nights) | 7 h 10 min (24/28 nights) |

Training duration increased 60 minutes, or 33%, relative to the previous weekly average. The longer hike is an important part of that change; it does not show improved running performance. Measured sleep was 30 minutes shorter at the median, though missing nights limit the comparison. We do not have subjective fatigue or soreness, so the effect on recovery remains uncertain.

Practical options: keep next week's schedule feasible before adding volume; describe how your legs felt after the hike so the next long session can be discussed in context; revisit sleep timing if the shorter nights reflect your actual routine. These are suggestions only; no workout was changed.

## 中文

**本周最明显的变化是周末单次运动更长，同时有记录的睡眠变短。**训练频率基本相同。下周加量前值得先看恢复，但这些数据不足以认定过度训练。

周期：2026 年 10 月 5–11 日，Asia/Shanghai。训练记录覆盖七天，睡眠覆盖六晚；对比周期为 9 月 7 日–10 月 4 日。不包含今天尚未结束的数据。

| 观察项 | 本周 | 此前四周 |
|---|---|---|
| 训练时长 | 240 分钟 | 平均每周 180 分钟 |
| 训练次数 | 4 次 | 平均每周 4 次 |
| 最长单次运动 | 徒步 120 分钟 | 每周最长单次时长的中位数为 75 分钟 |
| 有记录睡眠的中位数 | 6 小时 40 分（6/7 晚） | 7 小时 10 分（24/28 晚） |

相对此前每周平均，训练时长增加了 60 分钟，约 33%。较长的徒步是变化的重要组成部分，不能据此判断跑步能力提升。有记录睡眠的中位数缩短 30 分钟，但缺失晚数影响可比性。目前没有主观疲劳或酸痛反馈，恢复影响仍不确定。

可考虑：增加训练前先确认下周时间安排能否承受；补充徒步后的腿部感受，帮助讨论下一次长时间运动；如果短睡符合真实作息，再检查睡眠时间安排。以上只是建议，没有修改任何课程。
